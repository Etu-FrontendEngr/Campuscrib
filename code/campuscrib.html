Find A Space That Fits You

Explore a range of verified room options designed for comfort, privacy, and student living - all in one place.

Self Contain

Your own private space, no sharing. Comes with a bedroom, bathroom, and kitchen- perfect for comfort, independence, and peace of mind

Bedsitter

Simple, compact, and functional. A single-room space that combines your bedroom and living area, with a private bathroom - ideal for minimal living

One-Room Apartment

Affordable and practical. A private room setup with access to shared kitchen or facilities - a great option for students on a budget

2-Bedroom Apartment

More space, more flexibility. Perfect for sharing with a friend or having extra room, with separate bedrooms and shared living areas
